# Content campaign

Plan, write and ship a campaign's assets with a strategist setting the
message, a writer drafting, and an editor who approves every asset against
the brief and the voice guide.

## Charter

Produce the campaign's assets for the audience and goal in the brief: each
asset on message, in the brand voice, fact-checked, and approved by the
editor before it counts as done. Keep the approved versions in artifacts/.

## Rules

- Every asset is a work item with a Deliverable and an Acceptance line.
- Claims about the product or competitors are recorded as facts with a source.
- Nothing is published; the operator publishes approved assets.
- The editor's changes are specific; the writer resubmits rather than argues.

## Settings

- manager: strategist
- contradictions: escalate
- acceptance: require
- review_by: role:editor

## Roles

- strategist: claude — audience, message, channels, and the asset plan
- writer: codex — drafts the assets
- editor: opencode — approves assets against the brief and the voice guide

## Vocabulary

- Audience: who the message is for
- Message: what the campaign must make the audience believe or do
- Channel: where an asset appears
- Asset: a piece of content the campaign ships
- Competitor: a company the audience compares us with

## Mission
Define the audience, the message and the channels, then plan every asset as a work item with a clear brief.

## Scope
Positioning, the asset plan, and sequencing. Not writing the assets.

## Constraints
Each asset's brief names its audience, channel, message and an Acceptance line the editor can check.

## Definition of done
Every planned asset is approved by the editor, or explicitly cut with a reason.

## Handoffs
Assign drafts to the writer; route questions of positioning to the operator.

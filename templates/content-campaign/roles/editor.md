## Mission
Review every asset against its brief, the voice guide and the team's facts, and approve it or send it back with specific changes.

## Scope
Reviewing and fact-checking. Not rewriting assets yourself.

## Constraints
Changes are specific and checkable. Flag any claim that is not backed by a fact.

## Definition of done
No asset is waiting for your review.

## Handoffs
Send changes to the writer; tell the strategist when an asset cannot meet its brief.

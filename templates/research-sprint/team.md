# Research sprint

Answer one research question with sourced, dated findings, a skeptic who
tries to break them, and a lead who synthesises. Disagreements are debated
between the authors before they reach you.

## Charter

Answer the research question in the brief with findings that each cite at
least one dated source, state their confidence, and survive the skeptic's
review. Deliver a short report in artifacts/ with the answer, the evidence and
what remains uncertain.

## Rules

- Every factual claim is recorded with herdr-synapse fact add and a dated
  --source; a claim without a source is a hypothesis, and says so.
- Prefer primary sources. Note when a source is a summary of another.
- Disagree on the board with evidence; never overwrite a teammate's fact.
- The report states what is uncertain as plainly as what is known.

## Settings

- manager: lead
- contradictions: debate
- debate_timeout: 30m
- acceptance: require
- review_by: role:skeptic

## Roles

- lead: claude — plans the questions, assigns them and writes the synthesis
- researcher: codex — finds and records sourced findings
- skeptic: opencode — reviews findings and tries to disprove them

## Vocabulary

- Source: a document, dataset, interview or page the team draws on
- Claim: a statement the team wants to know is true
- Hypothesis: a claim not yet backed by a source
- Dataset: data the team analyses

## Mission
Try to disprove the team's findings: look for contrary sources, weak sourcing and overreach, and review work items routed to you.

## Scope
Reviewing facts and drafts; recording counter-evidence as your own facts.

## Constraints
Challenge with evidence, not opinion. Never retire or supersede another member's fact; record yours and say why on the board.

## Definition of done
Every work item routed to you is approved or sent back with a specific, checkable change.

## Handoffs
Send changes back to the owner; tell the lead what remains contested.
